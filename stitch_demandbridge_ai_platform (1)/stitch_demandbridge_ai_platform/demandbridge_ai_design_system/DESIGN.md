## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, enterprise-grade AI analytics interface bridging two foundational commercial sectors: agriculture producers and commercial retail operators. The visual personality balances the organic reliability and tangible yield of modern agricultural science with the surgical precision and forward-looking momentum of predictive machine learning.

The design movement is **Modern Corporate with Tactile-Analytical Precision**. It avoids both cold, sterile fintech tropes and folksy agrarian motifs. Instead, it pairs institutional rigor, high-density telemetry, and dense metric structures with humanized typographic clarity and breathable surfaces.

Key emotional and operational anchors:
- **Predictive Authority**: Interfaces convey deterministic confidence through explicit probability spreads, forecast confidence intervals, and crisp numerical visualization.
- **Dual-Persona Cohesion**: The system serves two symmetric user classes—producers ("Supply/Farm") and merchants ("Demand/Retail"). State changes switch context through deliberate visual anchors rather than disjointed themes.
- **Quiet Utility**: Interfaces prioritize raw data legibility, low cognitive fatigue during daylight field operation, and rapid micro-decisions in high-throughput retail purchasing contexts.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an organic core (Deep Emerald) calibrated with high-tech analytical accents (Precision Teal) anchored by an enterprise foundation (Slate Navy).

### Semantic Roles & Palette Application

- **Primary (`#0D5C3A` - Deep Emerald)**: Represents biological yield, verified supply chains, agricultural health, and primary positive actions (e.g., executing contracts, accepting supply matches).
- **Secondary (`#088395` - Precision Teal)**: Signals AI-derived logic, predictive curves, algorithmic certainty ratings, and smart recommendation flags.
- **Tertiary (`#0F1E36` - Slate Navy)**: Anchors global navigation, tabular headers, high-level fiscal aggregations, and high-impact structural framing.
- **Neutral (`#667085` - Slate Gray)**: Provides balanced optical contrast for metadata, microcopy, boundary rulers, and neutral trend trajectories.

### Supporting Canvas & Persona Tokens

- **Surface Neutral (Light)**: Canvas default is `#F8FAF9`, introducing a microscopic organic warmth to prevent stark clinical glare. Elevated cards utilize pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Farmer Persona Tint**: Subdued sage accents (`#EBF5F0` background, `#0D5C3A` border/text) demarcate inventory telemetry, crop stage yields, harvest schedules, and transport allocations.
- **Retailer Persona Tint**: Cool maritime accents (`#E6F4F7` background, `#088395` border/text) indicate shelf turnover rates, stockout alerts, store-level footfall demand, and spot pricing.
- **Algorithmic State Gradients**: Used sparingly—only for AI confidence meters and scenario simulators. Blends Deep Emerald into Precision Teal (`linear-gradient(135deg, #0D5C3A 0%, #088395 100%)`).
- **Telemetry Signals**: Critical stockout risk uses `#D92D20` (Warning Red), stable inventory uses `#079455` (Balanced Green), and pending machine-learning recalculations use `#F79009` (Amber).

## Typography

Typography pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural identity, headers, and KPI displays with **Inter** for high-density tabular records, dense data grids, form labels, and analytical narratives.

### Implementation Principles

- **Structural Rhythm**: Plus Jakarta Sans provides geometric geometric authority with human warmth. Tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) on larger display tiers prevents sprawling in dense dashboards.
- **Tabular Numerics**: Body text and metric labels in data grids must default to `font-feature-settings: 'tnum' on, 'cv05' on, 'zero' on` to guarantee absolute vertical column alignment for volatile metrics, monetary units, metric tons, and confidence percentages.
- **Micro-Labels**: `label-sm` is strictly uppercase for metric category headers (e.g., `CONFIDENCE BAND`, `HARVEST WINDOW`), tracking at `+0.04em` to preserve scannability at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptable **12-column responsive fluid grid system** backed by a strict 4px/8px mathematical increment ladder.

### Breakpoints & Grid Composition

- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12-column grid, max-width bounded to 1600px, 24px (`space-lg`) gutters, 32px (`space-xl`) outer margins. Supports side-by-side comparative split panes (Supply Stream vs Demand Projection).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column grid, 16px (`space-md`) gutters, 24px (`space-lg`) outer margins. KPI ribbons collapse into 2x2 or 4x1 wrapped cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid, 12px (`space-sm`) gutters, 16px (`space-md`) outer margins. Navigation collapses to a bottom dock or condensed app bar.

### Dashboard Architecture Rules

- Dense telemetry blocks (e.g., real-time crop yields, retail intake buffers) utilize internal compact cell padding of `space-sm` (12px) horizontally and `space-xs` (8px) vertically.
- Cards maintain an internal perimeter clearance of `space-lg` (24px) on desktop, reduced to `space-md` (16px) on mobile viewports.
- Spacing between disparate informational clusters (e.g., moving from Primary KPI Cards into Forecast Chart Engines) is locked to `space-2xl` (48px).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows and gimmicky blurred glassmorphism in favor of **refined micro-borders, tonal contrast layering, and crisp ambient occlusions**. This ensures maximum contrast under variable working conditions—such as bright sunlight in farm operations or fluorescent backroom retail lighting.

### Surface Tiers

1. **Canvas Level (Ground)**: `#F8FAF9`. Base canvas that houses all structural layout regions.
2. **Surface Low (Resting Containers)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on Canvas. Outlined with a 1px micro-border (`#E4E7EC`). Shadow: `0px 1px 2px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.04)`.
3. **Surface Mid (Interactive / Hover)**: Pure `#FFFFFF`. Micro-border intensifies to `#D0D5DD`. Shadow translates upward: `0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.08)`.
4. **Surface High (Drawers, Overlays, Command Center)**: Pure `#FFFFFF`. Micro-border: `#E4E7EC`. Shadow: `0px 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.12), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.04)`.

### Depth Accents

- **Ghost Framing**: All card separations prioritize 1px structural dividing lines in lieu of large drop shadows. Divider lines use `#EAECF0`.
- **Active Predictive Elevation**: When an AI recommendation is highlighted, the container does not cast a heavy shadow; instead, it adopts an internal 1.5px border tint of Precision Teal (`#088395`) alongside a light teal ambient glow: `0px 0px 0px 3px rgba(8, 131, 149, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework uses a **Soft (`1`) roundedness base** for structural components, combined with intentional pill geometry for high-priority status indicators. This preserves enterprise discipline and avoids bubbly or informal surfaces.

### Corner Radii Hierarchy

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px)**: Form input fields, checkbox containers, inline dropdown triggers, tabular row selection indicators.
- **Medium Radius (0.5rem / 8px)**: Standard KPI metric containers, analytical chart canvases, popovers, context menus, tooltips, standard buttons.
- **Large Radius (0.75rem / 12px)**: Primary parent modular cards, multi-stage transaction panels, persistent analytical viewports.
- **Pill Radius (9999px / Full)**: Exclusively reserved for status badges (e.g., `IN DEMAND`, `SURPLUS MATCH`), persona toggles (Farmer vs Retailer), numerical count chips, and primary floating action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Global)**: Solid Deep Emerald (`#0D5C3A`), white label text, 8px corner radius, height 40px, padding: 0 16px. Hover state deepens to `#09442B`. Focus ring: 3px subtle halo (`rgba(13, 92, 58, 0.20)`).
- **Secondary (AI Assist / Recommendation)**: Solid Precision Teal (`#088395`), white label text, 8px radius. Hover shifts to `#066472`. Used exclusively for triggering automated supply-match simulations or forecasting actions.
- **Outline / Tertiary**: 1px micro-border (`#D0D5DD`), white background, text Slate Navy (`#0F1E36`). Hover background `#F2F4F7`.

### KPI Metric & Demand Cards
- **Structure**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, 1px border (`#E4E7EC`), 8px radius, 20px internal padding.
- **Top Row**: 11px uppercase label (`label-sm`), muted neutral (`#667085`), paired with an optional right-aligned pill badge.
- **Center**: Numeric value rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` Bold (28px–32px) using tabular figures.
- **Bottom Row**: Micro-trend line or confidence badge. Trend up uses `#027A48` on `#ECFDF3`; trend down uses `#B42318` on `#FEF3F2`. 

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Format**: 24px height, padding 2px 10px, 9999px radius, typography `label-sm` (font size 11px, weight 600).
- **AI Recommendation Badge**: Subtle teal wash background (`#E6F4F7`), teal border (`#B3E0E7`), text `#088395`. Preceded by a 6px solid teal geometric dot or sparkle glyph.
- **Harvest Verified Badge**: Subtle emerald wash (`#EBF5F0`), border (`#A3D4BD`), text `#0D5C3A`.

### Persona Switcher (Farmer vs. Retailer State)
- Segmented pill track anchored in the primary application header.
- Background track `#EAECF0`, 4px internal padding, 9999px corner radius.
- Active item shifts with a sliding white pill with a 1px border (`#D0D5DD`) and soft shadow (`0px 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`).
- When "Farmer" is active, primary UI accents default to Deep Emerald. When "Retailer" is active, primary accents shift to Precision Teal.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Height 40px, 4px corner radius, 1px border (`#D0D5DD`), background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F1E36`.
- Active focus state: 1px border `#088395` accompanied by an analytical teal halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(8, 131, 149, 0.15)`.
- Input fields display unit indicators (e.g., `MT`, `Acres`, `Cases`, `Days`) in fixed right-aligned `data-mono` typography.

### Predictive Data Visualization & Charts
- **Line & Area Charts**: Historical historical curves use Slate Navy (`#0F1E36`, 2px solid). Predicted AI demand curves use Precision Teal (`#088395`, 2px dashed or solid).
- **Confidence Intervals**: Semi-transparent teal fill (`rgba(8, 131, 149, 0.10)`) framing the upper and lower predictive boundary limits.
- **Tooltips**: High-contrast Slate Navy dark tooltip card (`#0F1E36`), 6px radius, white text, showing timestamp, projected units, and machine learning confidence score (e.g., `94.8% accuracy index`).
